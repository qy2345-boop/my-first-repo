Summary

This archive brings together a series of browser-based computational studies exploring spatial, temporal, relational, and geospatial structures, as well as interactive engagement and agent-based systems. Each project uses code, data, and interaction to examine a different way of organizing, representing, and navigating information in the browser.

Number: 2026-TEMPORAL-03

Date: 2026/07/12

Type: D3.js / Temporal Structure + Agent

D3

When Do I Wake Up?

Temporal Structure + Agent

Apple Health sleep data is organized across nightly timelines and monthly calendars to examine how often awakenings occur, when they cluster, and which sleep stages usually precede them. An embedded agent extends the visualization by allowing patterns and anomalies in the sleep data to be queried directly.

Actors, Models, and Datasets

Relational Structure

An interactive D3.js network examining how actors, machine-learning models, filtering operations, and datasets participate in the construction of LAION-5B. Nodes can be selected and repositioned to isolate their direct relationships.

Number: 2026-GEOSPATIAL-05

Date: 2026/07/22

Type: Mapbox / Geospatial Structure + Engagement

MAP

Dark Kitchen Hunt

Geospatial Structure + Engagement Component

Food-delivery platform listings collected from five observation points are mapped through store locations, density, sales, category, and chain affiliation. The project combines geographic exploration with an engagement component that asks visitors to inspect suspected dark-kitchen clusters and contribute their own judgments.
